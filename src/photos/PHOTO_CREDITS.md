# Bundled boutique photos

Free-licensed storefront photographs from Wikimedia Commons, resized to a maximum edge of 800px and bundled in `src/photos/catalog/`. The user’s own photo always wins. These files are the fallback when the user has not added one. Each credit is also drawn on the image.

Only photographs that show the seeded address, or the same flagship building, are included. Everything else was skipped.

## Not bundled

No other seeded boutique had a Commons file that was clearly that storefront under CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA.

Skipped on purpose after checking the file:

- Chanel on Biblioteksgatan, Stockholm (`Vildmannen 7`) is a different address from Chanel Stockholm at Birger Jarlsgatan 7.
- Karl Johans gate 31, Oslo shows Louis Vuitton in the 2026 photograph, not the Hermès boutique in the catalogue.
- Hermès photographs of 24 Faubourg that only show the roofline were not used; the public-domain street-level storefront above is the one that shows the shop.
- Product close-ups (a scarf in a window, a Christmas bow) are not storefronts.
- Omega boutiques on Commons are other cities (Madrid, Budapest, Osaka, and others), not Biblioteksgatan, Old Bond Street, Østergade, Rue François 1er, Dubai Mall, or Karl Johans gate.
